For years, physiotherapy registration in India depended on where you practised. A few states had councils with working registers; in many others there was no clear statutory register at all, and "registration" often meant an association membership. The National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 2021 (NCAHP Act) set out to change that, and in 2026 the registration rules arrived.

This guide summarises what has been reported about the new rules, what they mean for a practising physiotherapist, and what you can sensibly do now. It is a plain-English summary, not legal advice. Always check the final text and your state council's instructions.

## What changed in 2026?

The **Registration of Allied and Healthcare Professionals Regulations, 2026** were published in the Gazette on **15 September 2026**. Physiotherapy is one of the professions covered by the NCAHP Act. On 7 October 2026 NCAHP also notified regulations for the autonomous boards, including the Ethics and Registration Board that verifies documents and maintains the state register.

In short: there is now a national framework for who can call themselves a registered physiotherapist, how that is checked, and how it is kept current.

## How registration will work

Based on published reports of the regulations:

- **You register through your state council.** The state council forwards your details to NCAHP for the **central register**. If your state does not yet have a functioning council, you can register directly on the central register.
- **One state, all-India practice.** You register in one state, based on where you live. You can practise anywhere in India, but you must notify your state council if you work outside your registered state.
- **Certificates carry a unique ID.** Certificates are issued online, carry a Unique Identification (UID) number and a QR code, and both the state and central registers are meant to be publicly verifiable.
- **A decision within a month.** State councils are to accept or reject an application within one month, give reasons in writing for a rejection, and you can appeal to the Commission.
- **Interns and graduates** doing their internship need temporary registration from the state council.
- **Indian nationals who studied abroad** register like everyone else. Foreign nationals can get temporary registration for study, clinical training and some other activities.
- **Provisional registration** is described for professionals without the full qualification, leading to full registration after a bridge course. Separate rules for this were still being drafted.

## Five-year validity and CPD

Registration is reported to be **valid for five years**, after which you renew. Renewal is tied to continuing professional development:

- **15 hours of CPD every year** (75 hours over the five-year cycle).
- Seminars, conferences, workshops, training programmes and faculty development programmes count.
- **No more than half of your hours can be online.** The Commission's stated aim is to discourage certificate-only compliance.

If you do not already track your CPD, start now: keep certificates, dates and hours in one folder. When renewal comes, you will want proof, not memory.

## Is IAP membership enough?

No. The Indian Association of Physiotherapists is the profession's national association and membership has real value: professional standing, conferences, networks and, for some, a route to overseas recognition. But it is **not** a statutory registration or a licence. Patients and employers will increasingly ask for your council or NCAHP registration number, and the public registers make it easy to check.

## When can you apply?

This is the honest answer: **not everywhere yet.** In September 2026 it was reported that registration would not start immediately, because the boards that process registrations were still being set up and the central portal was in a trial run, to be linked to state portals. NCAHP had earlier run an enrolment drive to collect data on professionals and institutions.

Practical steps for now:

1. **Gather your documents.** Degree certificate, mark sheets, internship completion certificate, identity and address proof, and any existing state council registration.
2. **If your state council already registers physiotherapists, register or renew there.** That number is what patients and employers can check today.
3. **Start logging CPD hours** in a way you can prove later.
4. **Watch for the portal opening** on the NCAHP website and your state council's notices, and be wary of agents charging to "fast-track" registration.
